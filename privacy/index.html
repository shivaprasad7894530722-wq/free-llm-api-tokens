Privacy policy

What this site collects, how cookies may be used for analytics and ads, and how you can opt out.

Last updated 12 Nov 2026.

Who is responsible for this site?

This website is published at https://freellmapi.link (the “site”). For privacy questions, email hello@lumiono.com. Do not include API keys or other secrets in that email.

What data does the site collect itself?

The public pages of this site do not ask you to create an account and do not include a form that collects names, passwords or API keys. If you email us, we will receive the address you sent from and the content of your message, and we will use that only to reply or to correct the list.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosts, Cloudflare may process technical request data such as IP addresses, user-agent strings and timestamps in order to serve the site, keep it secure and understand traffic. Retention follows Cloudflare’s then-current practices for that service.

Provider figures shown on the site come from public web pages and are stored in data.json. That file is not personal data about you.

Does this site use cookies?

The site itself stores your theme preference (light or dark) in your browser’s localStorage. That value stays on your device and is not sent to us as a login or tracking identifier.

Google Analytics 4 is active on this site and may set cookies or similar identifiers, as described below. Google AdSense is not active yet; its script is not loaded. If AdSense (or another advertising partner) is enabled later, those services may also set cookies, including advertising cookies.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used — for example, which pages are read and from which types of device. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technology and may collect identifiers, approximate location, device information and usage data. Google processes that data under its own policies.

You can learn more and review Google’s controls in Google’s Analytics privacy documentation and the Google Privacy Policy. Many browsers also let you block or delete cookies, and Google provides an opt-out browser add-on for some Analytics uses.

We do not use Analytics to collect API keys. Please do not paste keys into this site or into emails.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

We may use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ uses of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

The DoubleClick cookie

Google may use the DoubleClick cookie for AdSense ads served across the web, including on this site once AdSense is active. The DoubleClick cookie enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites. You can opt out of the DoubleClick cookie for personalised ads in Google Ads Settings and through the industry tool at www.aboutads.info.

Other third-party ad vendors

AdSense and similar networks may work with other third-party vendors that set cookies or collect request data in order to measure ads, limit how often you see them, or (where allowed) personalise them. Those vendors act as independent controllers or processors of that data under their own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

When AdSense is approved, this site’s ads.txt file will list the authorised advertising systems. Until a real publisher ID is entered, that file is a placeholder and does not authorise any seller.

Legal bases and retention (where those laws apply)

If you email us, we process the message in order to respond to your request. Server logs and security data are processed to operate and protect the site. Google Analytics is used to understand traffic. Advertising cookies will only be used if AdSense or another ad partner is enabled later. Where consent is required for non-essential cookies, we will only use those cookies in line with that consent tool once it is in place.

We keep email correspondence only as long as needed to deal with the request and any follow-up correction to the list. We do not have access to cookie stores on your device; you can delete those yourself in your browser.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

The tracker links to official provider sites and documentation. Those sites have their own privacy policies and terms. This policy applies only to https://freellmapi.link.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal data, cookies, Analytics or ads, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. The current version is the one published at this URL.
